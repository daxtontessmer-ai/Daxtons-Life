Picture Converter

Pick one or more pictures, like iPhone HEIC photos or screenshots, and convert them here. They stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Format

Download

    On iPhone, if the file opens instead of saving, tap the share button and choose Save to Files.

    Reads HEIC, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, TIFF, AVIF, and SVG. Only the first frame of an animated GIF is kept. HEIC output is not supported, because browsers can't make HEIC files. AVIF and WebP output depend on the browser; this page checks and turns them off when it can't make them. HEIC and TIFF pictures this browser can't open, GIF output, and ZIPs need a small helper downloaded once. iPhone: pictures over about 16 megapixels are scaled down to fit Safari's memory limit, and the page says when it does. This works in Safari on iPhone and in Chrome or Safari on a computer.
