Technical details

  
The first conversion downloads the converter (about 31 MB). After that it can run with no signal. Converting uses one processor core, so a full HD clip can take a few minutes. Leave this page open. iPhone: videos over 200 MB are turned away, because Safari closes the page when it runs out of memory. Computers can take videos up to about 1 GB. This works in Safari on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or newer) and in Chrome or Safari on a computer.
